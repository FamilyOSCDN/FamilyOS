# FamilyOS

> **The Digital Operating System for Modern Families**

FamilyOS is an open, modular and secure platform designed to help families organize, preserve and transmit every aspect of their family life.

Our vision is to build a digital ecosystem where memories, documents, knowledge, events and relationships remain accessible across generations.

---

# Vision

Every family creates valuable memories, documents, traditions and knowledge.

Unfortunately, this information is often scattered across phones, cloud services, paper archives and messaging applications.

FamilyOS aims to bring everything together into one secure platform that allows families to:

* Preserve memories
* Organize important documents
* Manage everyday family life
* Share information securely
* Build a digital family legacy
* Use AI as a trusted family assistant

---

# Project Goals

FamilyOS is built around several key principles:
