# 1. Purpose

This document defines the official engineering governance model for the
FamilyOS platform.

The objective is to establish how engineering decisions are created,
reviewed, approved, documented, and maintained throughout the lifecycle of
the platform.

---

# 2. Scope

This document applies to:

- architecture decisions;
- specifications;
- RFC processes;
- ADR processes;
- engineering standards;
- development practices;
- platform evolution.

---

# 3. Governance Principles

FamilyOS engineering governance SHALL follow:

- transparency;
- documentation;
- traceability;
- accountability;
- consistency;
- long-term sustainability.

---

# 4. Decision Framework

Engineering decisions SHALL be based on:

- documented requirements;
- technical analysis;
- architectural impact;
- security considerations;
- maintenance impact.

---

# 5. Engineering Documents

Engineering governance SHALL use:

| Document Type | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Specifications | Define expected behavior |
| RFCs | Propose new capabilities |
| ADRs | Record architectural decisions |
| ENG Documents | Define engineering standards |

---

# 6. RFC Governance

RFCs SHALL be used for significant changes.

An RFC SHOULD include:

- objective;
- motivation;
- proposed solution;
- alternatives;
- impact analysis;
- validation strategy.

---

# 7. ADR Governance

ADRs SHALL document important architectural decisions.

An ADR SHOULD record:

- context;
- decision;
- consequences;
- alternatives considered.

---

# 8. Review Process

Engineering decisions SHOULD be reviewed before approval.

Reviews SHOULD evaluate:

- technical correctness;
- architectural consistency;
- security impact;
- maintainability.

---

# 9. Responsibilities

Engineering responsibilities SHALL be clearly defined.

Responsibilities MAY include:

- proposing changes;
- reviewing documents;
- approving decisions;
- maintaining standards.

---

# 10. Change Management

Engineering changes SHALL consider:

- compatibility;
- documentation impact;
- operational impact;
- migration requirements.

---

# 11. Governance Evolution

Engineering governance SHOULD evolve with platform maturity.

Improvements SHOULD be based on:

- experience;
- feedback;
- metrics;
- lessons learned.

---

# 12. Compliance

All FamilyOS engineering decisions SHALL follow this governance model.

Exceptions SHALL be documented and approved.
