# 1. Purpose

This document defines the long-term engineering roadmap for the FamilyOS
platform.

The objective is to provide strategic direction for the evolution of
engineering practices, tooling, architecture, quality, and operational
capabilities.

---

# 2. Scope

This roadmap applies to:

- engineering processes;
- development practices;
- architecture evolution;
- quality improvement;
- automation;
- tooling;
- platform sustainability.

---

# 3. Roadmap Principles

The FamilyOS engineering roadmap SHALL prioritize:

- sustainable growth;
- technical excellence;
- continuous improvement;
- architectural integrity;
- developer productivity.

---

# 4. Engineering Evolution Areas

The roadmap is organized around:

| Area | Objective |
|---|---|
| Architecture | Improve system evolution and scalability |
| Development | Improve engineering workflows |
| Quality | Increase reliability and confidence |
| Automation | Reduce manual operations |
| Security | Strengthen platform protection |
| Developer Experience | Improve productivity |
| Operations | Improve platform reliability |

---

# 5. Short-Term Objectives

Short-term engineering objectives include:

- completing engineering documentation;
- strengthening validation workflows;
- improving automation;
- standardizing development practices;
- expanding quality frameworks.

---

# 6. Medium-Term Objectives

Medium-term objectives include:

- improving developer tooling;
- expanding CI/CD capabilities;
- increasing observability;
- improving performance measurement;
- strengthening plugin engineering.

---

# 7. Long-Term Objectives

Long-term engineering objectives include:

- scalable platform evolution;
- mature automation ecosystem;
- advanced operational capabilities;
- continuous architecture improvement;
- sustainable community contribution.

---

# 8. Architecture Evolution

Architecture evolution SHALL preserve:

- modularity;
- compatibility;
- maintainability;
- documented decisions.

Major changes SHALL follow RFC and ADR processes.

---

# 9. Engineering Maturity

FamilyOS engineering maturity SHOULD improve through:

- measurable progress;
- documented practices;
- automation;
- feedback loops.

---

# 10. Continuous Improvement

The engineering roadmap SHALL evolve with:

- platform needs;
- user requirements;
- technical discoveries;
- operational experience.

---

# 11. Governance

Roadmap changes SHALL be reviewed through the appropriate engineering
governance process.

---

# 12. Compliance

Engineering initiatives SHOULD align with this roadmap.

Exceptions SHALL be documented and approved.
