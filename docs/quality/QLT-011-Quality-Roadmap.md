# 1. Purpose

This document defines the long-term roadmap for the FamilyOS Quality
Framework.

The objective is to provide strategic direction for the evolution of quality
practices, governance, measurement, automation, and continuous improvement.

---

# 2. Scope

This roadmap applies to:

- quality processes;
- engineering practices;
- testing maturity;
- documentation quality;
- architecture quality;
- operational excellence.

---

# 3. Roadmap Principles

The Quality Roadmap SHALL prioritize:

- sustainable quality;
- measurable improvement;
- engineering excellence;
- automation;
- long-term platform health.

---

# 4. Quality Evolution Areas

The roadmap is organized around:

| Area | Objective |
|---|---|
| Code Quality | Improve implementation reliability |
| Architecture Quality | Preserve system integrity |
| Testing Quality | Increase validation confidence |
| Documentation Quality | Preserve knowledge |
| Process Quality | Improve workflows |
| Automation | Reduce manual effort |
| Governance | Maintain standards |

---

# 5. Short-Term Objectives

Short-term objectives include:

- completing quality documentation;
- establishing quality standards;
- improving validation workflows;
- strengthening quality gates.

---

# 6. Medium-Term Objectives

Medium-term objectives include:

- expanding quality automation;
- improving metrics collection;
- increasing audit maturity;
- improving engineering feedback loops.

---

# 7. Long-Term Objectives

Long-term objectives include:

- advanced quality intelligence;
- predictive quality analysis;
- mature engineering governance;
- continuous platform optimization.

---

# 8. Quality Automation Evolution

FamilyOS SHOULD improve automation for:

- compliance checks;
- documentation validation;
- quality measurements;
- release readiness.

---

# 9. Quality Maturity

Quality maturity SHOULD evolve through:

- defined standards;
- measurable practices;
- continuous improvement;
- organizational learning.

---

# 10. Ecosystem Quality

As the FamilyOS ecosystem grows, quality practices SHALL support:

- official plugins;
- community contributions;
- compatibility;
- long-term stability.

---

# 11. Governance

Quality roadmap changes SHALL follow FamilyOS governance processes.

Major changes SHOULD be documented through appropriate mechanisms.

---

# 12. Compliance

All quality initiatives SHOULD align with this roadmap.

Exceptions SHALL be documented and approved.
