# 1. Purpose

This document defines the official process quality standards for the FamilyOS
platform.

The objective is to ensure that engineering and operational processes remain
effective, consistent, measurable, and continuously improving.

---

# 2. Scope

This document applies to:

- development processes;
- review processes;
- documentation processes;
- testing processes;
- release processes;
- governance workflows.

---

# 3. Process Quality Principles

FamilyOS processes SHALL prioritize:

- clarity;
- consistency;
- efficiency;
- traceability;
- continuous improvement.

---

# 4. Process Definition

Processes SHALL define:

- objectives;
- responsibilities;
- required activities;
- expected outcomes;
- validation criteria.

---

# 5. Process Consistency

FamilyOS processes SHOULD remain consistent across teams and components.

Consistency SHOULD apply to:

- workflows;
- documentation;
- approvals;
- validation steps.

---

# 6. Process Effectiveness

Processes SHOULD provide measurable value.

Process evaluation SHOULD consider:

- efficiency;
- reliability;
- execution effort;
- improvement opportunities.

---

# 7. Process Documentation

Processes SHALL be documented.

Documentation SHOULD include:

- purpose;
- scope;
- workflow;
- responsibilities;
- compliance requirements.

---

# 8. Process Automation

Processes SHOULD be automated when practical.

Automation MAY improve:

- repeatability;
- validation;
- reporting;
- productivity.

---

# 9. Process Compliance

Processes SHALL follow approved governance rules.

Compliance SHOULD be verified through:

- reviews;
- audits;
- automated checks.

---

# 10. Process Improvement

Processes SHALL evolve over time.

Improvements SHOULD be based on:

- metrics;
- feedback;
- incidents;
- lessons learned.

---

# 11. Process Risks

Process risks SHOULD be identified and managed.

Examples:

- unclear responsibilities;
- unnecessary complexity;
- missing validation;
- inefficient workflows.

---

# 12. Compliance

All FamilyOS processes SHALL follow these quality standards.

Exceptions SHALL be documented and approved.
