# 1. Purpose

This document defines the official quality lifecycle for the FamilyOS
platform.

The lifecycle establishes the required phases for defining, applying,
validating, measuring, and improving quality throughout the platform
lifecycle.

---

# 2. Scope

This lifecycle applies to all FamilyOS activities, including:

- architecture;
- development;
- testing;
- documentation;
- releases;
- operations;
- plugin ecosystem management.

---

# 3. Quality Lifecycle Principles

The FamilyOS quality lifecycle SHALL be:

- continuous;
- measurable;
- documented;
- integrated with engineering practices.

---

# 4. Quality Lifecycle Overview

Quality SHALL follow these phases:

| Phase | Description |
|---|---|
| 1 | Quality Definition |
| 2 | Quality Planning |
| 3 | Quality Implementation |
| 4 | Quality Validation |
| 5 | Quality Measurement |
| 6 | Quality Improvement |

---

# 5. Quality Definition

The first phase defines expected quality standards.

This phase SHALL identify:

- requirements;
- quality objectives;
- acceptance criteria;
- applicable standards.

---

# 6. Quality Planning

Quality planning SHALL determine:

- validation methods;
- required tests;
- review processes;
- measurement approach.

---

# 7. Quality Implementation

Quality SHALL be integrated into implementation activities.

This includes:

- engineering standards;
- coding practices;
- architecture rules;
- documentation requirements.

---

# 8. Quality Validation

Quality validation SHOULD include:

- automated testing;
- code reviews;
- architecture reviews;
- compliance checks.

---

# 9. Quality Measurement

Quality SHALL be evaluated through meaningful indicators.

Measurements MAY include:

- defect trends;
- test results;
- technical debt;
- documentation status;
- reliability indicators.

---

# 10. Quality Improvement

Quality improvements SHOULD be continuous.

Improvement activities MAY include:

- process refinement;
- automation enhancement;
- architecture improvements;
- documentation updates.

---

# 11. Quality Integration

The Quality Lifecycle integrates with:

| Framework | Relationship |
|---|---|
| Engineering | Defines implementation practices |
| Testing | Provides validation confidence |
| Documentation | Preserves knowledge |
| Release | Ensures delivery quality |

---

# 12. Quality Reviews

Quality reviews SHOULD occur at appropriate lifecycle stages.

Reviews MAY evaluate:

- compliance;
- risks;
- improvements;
- readiness.

---

# 13. Compliance

All FamilyOS quality activities SHALL follow this lifecycle.

Exceptions SHALL be documented and approved.
