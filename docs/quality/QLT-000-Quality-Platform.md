# 1. Purpose

This document defines the foundation of the FamilyOS Quality Platform.

It establishes the principles, structures, and objectives required to
maintain software quality, engineering excellence, reliability, and
continuous improvement throughout the FamilyOS lifecycle.

The Quality Platform provides the foundation for all quality activities
across FamilyOS.

---

# 2. Scope

This document applies to all FamilyOS components, including:

- Core Platform;
- Runtime;
- CLI;
- SDK;
- Domain Framework;
- Generation Framework;
- Official Plugins;
- Community Plugins;
- Infrastructure;
- Documentation;
- Build and Release systems.

---

# 3. Quality Platform Objectives

The Quality Platform SHALL ensure:

- consistent quality practices;
- measurable improvement;
- reliable delivery;
- sustainable evolution;
- controlled technical growth.

---

# 4. Quality Foundation

The FamilyOS Quality Platform is based on:

## 4.1 Quality by Design

Quality SHALL be considered from the beginning of development.

Quality considerations SHALL exist during:

- requirements;
- architecture;
- implementation;
- testing;
- release.

---

## 4.2 Continuous Validation

Quality SHALL be continuously evaluated.

Validation MAY include:

- automated checks;
- reviews;
- testing;
- metrics;
- audits.

---

## 4.3 Measurable Improvement

Quality improvements SHOULD be based on:

- evidence;
- metrics;
- feedback;
- identified risks.

---

## 4.4 Sustainable Quality

Quality SHALL prioritize long-term platform health.

Short-term solutions SHOULD NOT create unnecessary future complexity.

---

# 5. Quality Domains

The Quality Platform includes:

| Domain | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Code Quality | Maintainable and reliable implementation |
| Architecture Quality | Sustainable system design |
| Documentation Quality | Reliable knowledge management |
| Process Quality | Effective engineering practices |
| Security Quality | Protection and risk management |
| Release Quality | Reliable delivery |

---

# 6. Quality Management Model

FamilyOS quality follows:

1. Define quality expectations
2. Apply engineering standards
3. Validate implementation
4. Measure results
5. Improve continuously

---

# 7. Quality Governance

Quality activities SHALL follow:

- engineering governance;
- documented standards;
- validation processes;
- continuous improvement practices.

---

# 8. Quality Automation

Automation SHOULD support quality management.

Automation MAY include:

- static analysis;
- testing validation;
- documentation checks;
- compliance verification.

---

# 9. Quality Culture

FamilyOS quality SHALL be considered a shared responsibility.

Every engineering activity SHOULD contribute to maintaining quality.

---

# 10. Evolution

The Quality Platform SHALL evolve with FamilyOS maturity.

Improvements SHOULD be guided by:

- metrics;
- audits;
- engineering feedback;
- platform evolution.

---

# 11. Compliance

All FamilyOS components SHALL follow the Quality Platform principles.

Exceptions SHALL be documented and approved.
