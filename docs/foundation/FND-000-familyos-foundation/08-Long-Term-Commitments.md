# Long-Term Commitments

## Purpose

This document defines the enduring commitments of the FamilyOS project.

These commitments express the long-term responsibilities that the project accepts toward its users, contributors, maintainers, and the broader engineering community.

Unlike implementation strategies or engineering practices, these commitments are intended to remain stable throughout the lifetime of the project.

---

# Commitment Philosophy

FamilyOS is designed as a long-lived engineering platform.

Every major decision SHOULD strengthen the platform's ability to evolve without compromising its architectural integrity, engineering quality, or accumulated knowledge.

Long-term sustainability SHALL always take precedence over short-term convenience.

---

# Commitment 1 — Preserve the Vision

FamilyOS SHALL remain aligned with the vision defined by the Foundation.

Future evolution MUST strengthen rather than contradict the project's mission, values, and engineering philosophy.

Major strategic changes SHOULD be carefully evaluated against the Foundation before adoption.

---

# Commitment 2 — Preserve Architectural Integrity

Architecture is considered one of the project's most valuable assets.

FamilyOS SHALL:

* preserve clear architectural boundaries;
* maintain separation of concerns;
* avoid unnecessary coupling;
* document significant architectural evolution.

Architectural consistency SHALL remain a long-term priority.

---

# Commitment 3 — Preserve Engineering Quality

Engineering quality SHALL never be treated as optional.

The project is committed to:

* continuous improvement;
* engineering discipline;
* sustainable implementation practices;
* maintainable software;
* high documentation quality.

Quality is considered a permanent responsibility.

---

# Commitment 4 — Preserve Knowledge

Engineering knowledge SHALL be documented and maintained.

Important knowledge MUST NOT depend solely on individual contributors.

FamilyOS is committed to preserving:

* architectural decisions;
* technical designs;
* engineering standards;
* implementation requirements;
* lessons learned.

Knowledge preservation is essential for long-term sustainability.

---

# Commitment 5 — Maintain Documentation

Documentation SHALL evolve together with the platform.

The project commits to maintaining documentation that is:

* accurate;
* complete;
* reviewable;
* version controlled;
* traceable.

Outdated documentation SHOULD be revised or formally deprecated.

---

# Commitment 6 — Encourage Sustainable Evolution

FamilyOS is designed to evolve continuously.

Evolution SHOULD:

* preserve compatibility whenever practical;
* minimize unnecessary disruption;
* maintain architectural consistency;
* support gradual improvement.

Technical debt SHOULD be actively managed rather than ignored.

---

# Commitment 7 — Protect Security and Privacy

Security and privacy are enduring project responsibilities.

FamilyOS SHALL continue to improve:

* secure design;
* secure implementation;
* secure engineering practices;
* privacy protection;
* transparency regarding security-related decisions.

Security SHALL remain integrated into every stage of the engineering lifecycle.

---

# Commitment 8 — Support Contributors

FamilyOS is committed to providing contributors with:

* understandable architecture;
* comprehensive documentation;
* consistent engineering standards;
* transparent governance;
* predictable contribution processes.

The project recognizes that contributor experience directly influences long-term sustainability.

---

# Commitment 9 — Promote Openness

Whenever appropriate, engineering knowledge SHOULD remain open, reviewable, and understandable.

The project encourages:

* constructive collaboration;
* transparent communication;
* evidence-based discussions;
* documented technical decisions.

Openness contributes to engineering excellence.

---

# Commitment 10 — Build for Generations

FamilyOS is intended to outlive individual technologies, implementation choices, and contributors.

The platform SHALL therefore prioritize:

* adaptability;
* maintainability;
* documentation quality;
* architectural stability;
* knowledge continuity.

The ultimate objective is to create a platform capable of serving families across generations.

---

# Long-Term Success

FamilyOS considers its long-term commitments fulfilled when:

* the platform remains understandable after many years;
* contributors can safely evolve the system;
* documentation accurately reflects the software;
* engineering knowledge is preserved;
* architectural integrity is maintained;
* users continue to trust the platform.

These outcomes define long-term success more accurately than short-term feature delivery.

---

# Relationship to Other Foundation Documents

The Foundation establishes:

* the Vision that defines the future direction of FamilyOS;
* the Mission that explains its purpose;
* the Core Values that guide decisions;
* the Engineering Philosophy that shapes engineering practices;
* the Architecture Principles that govern system design;
* the Documentation Principles that preserve knowledge;
* the Governance model that organizes project evolution.

This document concludes the normative Foundation by expressing the enduring commitments that FamilyOS accepts for the lifetime of the project.

Every future ADR, RFC, SPEC, and ENG document SHOULD remain consistent with these commitments unless a future revision of the Foundation explicitly modifies them.
