# RFC-0003 — Plugin Discovery & Distribution

# 22 — Decisions

## Decision 1 — Repository-Based Distribution

FamilyOS will use repositories as the foundation for plugin distribution.

## Decision 2 — Separation of Concerns

Discovery, distribution, installation, verification, and execution remain separate responsibilities.

## Decision 3 — Manifest-Based Plugins

Every distributed plugin must provide a standardized manifest.

## Decision 4 — Lifecycle Management

Plugin lifecycle operations are first-class platform capabilities.

## Decision 5 — Extensible Architecture

The design must support future marketplace and enterprise scenarios without redesigning the core.
