# Quality Framework

# 01 Context

## Overview

Software quality is a fundamental requirement for the long-term evolution of the FamilyOS platform.

As FamilyOS grows in complexity, with multiple domains, plugins, frameworks, and engineering contributors, quality must be managed as a structured and permanent capability.

The Quality Framework exists to establish a common understanding of quality expectations and provide a foundation for consistent engineering practices.

---

# Background

FamilyOS is designed as an extensible engineering platform built around:

* Clean Architecture principles;
* Domain-driven design;
* modular plugin architecture;
* documented engineering processes;
* long-term maintainability.

As the platform evolves, isolated quality practices are no longer sufficient.

A unified quality model is required to ensure that all components follow consistent expectations.

---

# Current Situation

Before the Quality Framework, quality practices were distributed across multiple engineering activities.

Quality-related concerns existed within:

* development practices;
* testing activities;
* documentation processes;
* build operations;
* release preparation.

However, these capabilities required a common framework to define their relationships and responsibilities.

---

# Problem Statement

Without a unified Quality Framework, FamilyOS could face:

* inconsistent quality expectations;
* unclear validation responsibilities;
* reduced traceability;
* duplicated quality practices;
* difficulty measuring improvement.

A growing engineering ecosystem requires explicit quality governance.

---

# Quality Framework Motivation

The Quality Framework provides a structured approach to:

* define quality principles;
* establish quality governance;
* integrate validation activities;
* support engineering decisions;
* enable continuous improvement.

Quality becomes a shared engineering responsibility.
