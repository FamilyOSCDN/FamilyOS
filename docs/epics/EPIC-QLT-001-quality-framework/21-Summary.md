# Quality Framework

# 21 Summary

## Overview

EPIC-QLT-001 — Quality Framework establishes the official quality foundation for the FamilyOS ecosystem.

The framework defines how quality is understood, governed, evaluated, and continuously improved throughout the complete engineering lifecycle.

Quality becomes a permanent engineering capability rather than a final verification activity.

---

# Mission

The mission of the Quality Framework is to enable FamilyOS to evolve with confidence by establishing:

* consistent quality practices;
* clear engineering expectations;
* measurable improvement direction;
* reliable decision processes;
* sustainable quality governance.

---

# Core Capabilities

The Quality Framework introduces several fundamental capabilities.

## Quality Principles

Defines the principles that guide engineering quality decisions.

Key concepts:

* Quality By Design;
* Prevention Over Correction;
* Evidence-Based Quality;
* Continuous Improvement;
* Shared Ownership.
