# Quality Framework

# 18 Roadmap

## Overview

The Quality Framework roadmap defines the planned evolution of quality capabilities within the FamilyOS ecosystem.

The objective is to progressively improve quality maturity through structured practices, automation, measurement, and continuous improvement.

The roadmap follows the principle that quality capabilities should evolve together with platform complexity.

---

# Roadmap Objectives

The Quality Framework roadmap aims to establish:

* consistent quality practices;
* measurable quality improvements;
* automated validation capabilities;
* stronger engineering confidence;
* sustainable quality governance.

---

# Quality Maturity Evolution

FamilyOS quality maturity evolves through progressive stages.

```text
Quality Awareness

        ↓

Defined Quality Practices

        ↓

Automated Quality Validation

        ↓

Measured Quality Management

        ↓

Continuous Quality Engineering
```

---

# Phase 1 — Quality Foundation

## Objective

Establish the fundamental quality model.

Capabilities:

* Quality Framework documentation;
* quality principles;
* governance foundations;
* lifecycle definition;
* relationship with existing frameworks.

Expected outcome:

A shared understanding of quality across the ecosystem.

---

# Phase 2 — Quality Standards

## Objective

Create reusable and consistent quality practices.

Capabilities:

* quality standards;
* contribution guidelines;
* validation expectations;
* engineering checklists;
* quality documentation patterns.

Expected outcome:

Consistent quality expectations across projects and plugins.

---

# Phase 3 — Quality Automation

## Objective

Increase automated quality feedback.

Capabilities:

* automated quality checks;
* validation workflows;
* quality reports;
* CI integration;
* automated Quality Gates.

Expected outcome:

Faster and more reliable quality feedback.

---

# Phase 4 — Quality Measurement

## Objective

Introduce measurable quality management.

Capabilities:

* quality indicators;
* trend analysis;
* engineering metrics;
* quality dashboards;
* improvement tracking.

Expected outcome:

Better visibility into ecosystem quality.

---

# Phase 5 — Continuous Quality Engineering

## Objective

Create a mature quality-driven engineering ecosystem.

Capabilities:

* proactive risk identification;
* intelligent quality assistance;
* advanced governance;
* ecosystem-wide optimization.

Expected outcome:

Quality becomes an integrated engineering advantage.
