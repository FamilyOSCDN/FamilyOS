# Quality Framework

# 17 Quality Lifecycle

## Overview

The Quality Lifecycle defines how quality is created, maintained, evaluated, and improved throughout the complete FamilyOS engineering lifecycle.

Quality is not a single activity performed at the end of development.

Quality is a continuous process integrated into every stage of platform evolution.

---

# Purpose Of The Quality Lifecycle

The Quality Lifecycle ensures that quality remains present during:

* planning;
* architecture;
* development;
* validation;
* delivery;
* operation;
* improvement.

It provides a continuous approach to maintaining engineering confidence.

---

# Quality Lifecycle Model

FamilyOS quality follows a continuous lifecycle.

```text
Planning

    ↓

Design

    ↓

Implementation

    ↓

Validation

    ↓

Delivery

    ↓

Observation

    ↓

Improvement

    ↓

Planning
```

The cycle continuously evolves with the platform.

---

# Planning Phase

Quality begins during planning.

Quality considerations include:

* objectives;
* expected outcomes;
* risks;
* constraints;
* validation requirements.

Early quality decisions reduce future problems.

---

# Design Phase

Architecture and design decisions strongly influence quality.

Quality considerations include:

* maintainability;
* scalability;
* simplicity;
* testability;
* security;
* documentation needs.

Good design creates quality foundations.

---

# Implementation Phase

During implementation, contributors apply quality principles.

Expected practices include:

* coding standards;
* controlled dependencies;
* appropriate validation;
* documentation updates;
* responsible decisions.

---

# Validation Phase

Validation provides evidence that quality expectations are met.

Activities may include:

```text
Automated Checks

        ↓

Testing

        ↓

Review

        ↓

Quality Assessment
```

Validation supports informed decisions.

---

# Delivery Phase

Delivery requires confidence in the produced artifacts.

Quality considerations include:

* build validation;
* artifact verification;
* release readiness;
* documentation completeness.

---

# Observation Phase

After delivery, quality continues through observation.

Feedback may come from:

* operational experience;
* user feedback;
* maintenance activities;
* discovered risks.

Observation provides improvement opportunities.

---

# Improvement Phase

Quality improves through continuous learning.

Improvements may include:

* better processes;
* stronger automation;
* updated standards;
* improved documentation;
* reduced technical debt.

---

# Quality Feedback Loop

FamilyOS uses feedback to improve continuously.

```text
Engineering Experience

        ↓

Quality Feedback

        ↓

Process Improvement

        ↓

Better Engineering Practices
```
